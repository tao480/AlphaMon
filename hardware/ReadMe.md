The AlphaMon Platform firmware was originally developed on one of the early board entrants to the ESP32 market, namely the Heltec ESP32 LoRa V3.

Since that release, many new ESP32 boards have entered the market. However, the M5Stack Tab5 is a new, all-in-one entrant that is particularly well
packaged and visually attractive enclosure.  Because of its neat packaging it was quickly adopted in the Japanese developer market where it has
been very successful.

The M5Stack company was acquired by Expessif (the creators of the ESP32 chip family) in 2024 which ensures strong compatibility and support, although
the two companies continue to operate independently.

The AlphaMon firmware is currently being ported to the Tab5 and is expected to be an outstanding addition to the AlphaMon family, particularly as 
a fully integrated, indoor display of the AlphaMon's energy data, suiting either table-top use or wall mounted formats.

The Tab5's integrated 5″ 1280×720 IPS touchscreen, full color display and extensive range of add-on modules, including the M5 LoRa module, make it 
ideal for home and office use. 

See: https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/Tab5 for the product overview and technical information.
